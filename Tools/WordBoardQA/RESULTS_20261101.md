# SPELLSTRIKE board-solvability benchmark, 2026-11-01

**Scope:** 200 seeded starting boards per generic stage label (1-4), nine test conditions each. 800 base boards and 7,200 board evaluations. Seed 20261101. Read-only, offline model of normal combat generator.

**Dictionary:** 21,004 primary entries with definitions; 32,887 extra accepted fallback entries; 53,891 unique accepted combat words.

Every evaluated board had at least one valid short word. The weakest observed board still had 61 recognized words, though that is a technical count, not a claim that a human player will know them. This is NOT a mathematical dead-end guarantee for all RNG states.

The main risk is long-word accessibility. Four locks reduce the median accepted-word count by over 60%, and 618/800 such samples have no currently formable 10+ letter word. Four locks means two calls to TileDebuffManager.ApplyTileLocking (one call caps at two); real combat recovery timing may make this rarer.

Using only primary definition-backed vocabulary, the initial median is 592.5 formable words, dropping to 224.0 after four locks. Two four-lock cases and three emergency re-roll cases had no formable 6-9 letter primary word. Note: having a definition does NOT guarantee a word is age-appropriate or familiar.

## Method and exclusions

The analyzer mirrors the generic TileManager normal combat initial-pool planner (random playable 9-13-letter primary word plus an exact-length primary complement), balanced vowel/consonant emergency fallback, and word-budget refill. It checks the combined DictionaryManager primary and filtered fallback lists. Every simulated selected tile is counted once, with no adjacency requirement. Locked tiles are removed from the usable multiset; cracked tiles remain selectable.

- Small replacement scenarios (1-2 tiles) are deliberate stress cases, not normal 3+ letter Attack submissions.
- 3-5 and 6-9 consumption scenarios select random currently valid words from the chosen tier, not a player-optimal strategy.
- Full emergency re-roll is based on SPELLSTRIKE's balanced fallback generator, NOT the original Bookworm RNG.
- Stage 2 maritime objectives and Stage 3 authored riddle overrides depend on live quest state and were NOT simulated. The stage numbers here are independent generic seed samples, NOT verified distinct difficulty curves.
- This is not an actual Unity TileManager invocation or a live Play Mode test. Unity was already in the user's Play Mode session and was not disturbed. No Android device was used.
- Extra word coverage was NOT added to the game. The published BA2 word list remains in a research folder outside the Unity project.

## Recommendations

1. Keep current normal-combat generation unchanged for now. These 7,200 seeded evaluations found no dead boards.
2. Add future runtime guardrails for zero valid basic words, zero definition-backed common choices, and persistent 10+ letter droughts. A naive full dictionary scan every frame would be too costly for Android.
3. QA real stage 2 maritime/10-letter objectives and stage 3 riddles in isolated Unity Play Mode sessions.
4. Test 10-turn adversarial sequences with repeated debuffs and very small refills; a one-step benchmark cannot prove that players never get stuck over multiple turns.
5. If expanding accepted vocabulary, curate age suitability and licensing, and keep words eligible for tile generation separate from the larger accepted guess dictionary.

# SPELLSTRIKE Magic Orb VFX

## Purpose
The cyan-white Basic Attack projectile visual is derived from the Gabriel Aguiar `Unity VFX Graph - Magic Orb Effect Tutorial` reference and enhanced with Hovl's `Magic Effects FREE` particle assets. It is intended to replace the plain Basic Attack projectile sphere while preserving the existing homing, collision, damage, and word-submission behavior.

## Production visual assets
- `MagicOrb_HovlBasicAttack.prefab` is the visual prefab nested by the production projectile. It contains the bright VFX Graph Core and Motes, Hovl's cyan spherical shield contour, a strengthened radial additive halo, a camera-facing additive mesh aura, and controlled blue travel sparks.
- `Scripts/VFX/MagicOrbGlowBillboard.cs` adds a small camera-facing glow mesh using `BasicChargeEnergyMobileSafe.shader`. It is attached to `MagicOrb_HovlBasicAttack.prefab`, so the production projectile receives it automatically. The quad stays behind the core, follows `Camera.main`, and uses only four vertices/six indices; it does not require HDR, bloom, or a ParticleSystem material.
- The `Hovl_SoftSphericalGlow` particle was tuned from size 2.55 / alpha 0.48 to size 2.9 / alpha 0.66, with its screen-size cap reduced to 0.40 to keep the aura controlled. The additional mesh aura is local diameter 0.22 at the VFX prefab level (1.65 world units under the production visual's 7.5 scale), color `(0.06, 0.42, 1.0, 0.5)`, and 0.08-world-unit offset behind the core.
- `OrbCore.vfx` is the bright cyan-white central core; `OrbMotes.vfx` supplies the surrounding motes.
- `Shell` (the original VFX Graph shell layer) is intentionally inactive in `MagicOrb_HovlBasicAttack.prefab`. In the current URP Game view it produced a dark quadrilateral/diamond artifact. Keep it disabled unless the graph is corrected and revalidated.
- `Materials/MagicOrb_SoftRadialGlow.asset` and `Materials/MagicOrb_SoftRadialGlow_Additive.mat` provide the transparent radial falloff used by the halo and sparks. This avoids the square/opaque appearance of the stock Hovl `Point` texture/material.
- Hovl contour source: `Assets/Hovl Studio/Magic effects pack/Prefabs/Magic shields/Magic shield blue.prefab`. The package source prefabs/materials were not modified by the orb-specific tuning.
- `Impact/PlayerMagicOrb_ElectroHit_OneShot.prefab` is the separate cyan Electro hit asset. It contains one deterministic root burst (cleanup owner), one radial glow burst, and 16 radial-additive sparks. The root ParticleSystem is non-looping with `Stop Action = Destroy`.
- `Variants/` retains the electric/shield/spark candidates and the comparison scene for future iteration. These are not all production assets.

## Gameplay integration state
- `Assets/Prefabs/PlayerProjectile_MagicOrb.prefab` retains its root `HomingProjectile`, trigger `SphereCollider`, kinematic `Rigidbody`, and disabled plain-sphere `MeshRenderer`. Its `Magic Orb Visual` child now nests `MagicOrb_HovlBasicAttack.prefab`.
- `Assets/Prefabs/VanAertPlayer.prefab` references `Assets/Prefabs/PlayerProjectile_MagicOrb.prefab` through `AttackController.playerProjectile`.
- The four attack clips in `Assets/VAN AERT/VanANIMCONTROLLER.controller` carry `OnAttackRelease` at Basic 0.833s, Medium 0.633s, Strong 1.267s, and Ultimate 1.000s; each clip also has `OnAttackAnimationFinished` near its end. BasicAttack now additionally has `OnAttackChargeStart` at frame 0. Its existing release stays at frame 25 (0.833s) and finish at frame 68 (2.25s). `OnAttackRelease()` still calls `ShootMagicProjectile()`, which instantiates the production projectile at `MagicSpawnPoint` and calls `HomingProjectile.Launch()`.
- `Assets/Scripts/VFX/BasicAttackChargeBinding.cs` on the canonical player receives `OnAttackChargeStart` and gates the charge presentation until that event. It samples the existing BasicAttack Animator state for progress, attaches the reusable `Assets/VFX/BasicAttackCharge/BasicAttackCharge.prefab` to `MagicSpawnPoint`, and fades its presentation for 0.16 clip-time seconds after the frame-25 release. It does not send gameplay events or affect projectile/damage authority.
- `Assets/Scripts/VFX/BasicAttackChargeVfx.cs` now grows continuously from its serialized `startSize` (0.18) toward full size with `growthPower` (1.35), while the existing energy curve brightens the cyan-white core. This restores the documented small/faint-to-larger/brighter direction; the stale compression implementation was removed.
- Runtime gameplay logs confirmed the animation event reached `ShootMagicProjectile()` and spawned/launched the production projectile. The staff spawn marker is attached to the staff mesh near its local tip (mesh Y max 1.88; marker local Y 1.79).
- The production projectile's `MagicOrb Visual` child is now scaled to 7.5 (three times its previous 2.5 scale) without changing the root collider. `HomingProjectile` holds movement for 0.12 seconds after `Launch()` so the orb is visibly present at the staff release point before homing movement begins; homing/target selection is unchanged after that brief visibility window.
- Impact VFX is now enemy-owned rather than projectile-owned. `EnemyHealth.TakeDamage`, `EnemyController.TakeDamage`, and `BrambleSpriteController.TakeDamage` call `Spellstrike.Vfx.EnemyDamageImpactTrigger.PlayFor(transform)` only when `beforeHP - afterHP > 0`.
- The damage receiver lazily creates/reuses a child GameObject named `Damage Impact VFX Trigger`. That object loads `Resources/Spellstrike/VFX/BasicAttackImpactMeshBurst.prefab`, prioritizes solid body colliders over aggro/range trigger volumes, finds the camera-facing body surface, and spawns the visual 0.25 units outside it. A bounds-support fallback is used if the camera ray does not intersect a collider.
- `BasicAttackImpactVfx` creates one mobile-safe additive mesh with a white-cyan core, expanding cyan ring, and outward streaks. It does not rely on ParticleSystem materials, post-processing, or bloom. The effect is unparented from the enemy and self-destroys after 0.58 seconds, so lethal damage does not delete the burst with the enemy.
- The legacy `Impact/PlayerMagicOrb_ElectroHit_OneShot.prefab` remains in the project as an unused asset; it is no longer the production impact path. `HomingProjectile` still owns accepted-target checks, damage, vocabulary barriers, Target Challenge resolution, owner notification, and projectile cleanup.
- Charge-up is presentation-only. No projectile launch timing, damage calculation, vocabulary barrier resolution, Target Challenge matching, owner notification, or Spellbook behavior was changed.
- Medium Attack opts into `HomingProjectile.EnableMediumTwinVisuals()` after the normal single `Launch()` call. `MediumTwinOrbPresentation` creates two visual-only instances of `MagicOrb_HovlBasicAttack.prefab` and disables the original single visual only on that Medium Attack projectile instance. Its serialized AnimationCurve is keyed at 0% = 0 units, 50% = 13 units, 70% = 14 units, and 100% = 0 units of lateral offset per orb. The first leg is fast, the 50–70% outward sweep is slow, and the 70–100% return accelerates inward like a synchronized twin-missile strike. That yields 26 units of total orb-center separation at 50% and 28 units at 70%.
- Path progress is driven by the single root projectile's remaining distance to the same target; the mirrored paths close during the final 2.5-unit approach. The two visual children have no colliders. The projectile root retains exactly one `HomingProjectile`, one trigger `SphereCollider` (radius 0.5), and its existing target/damage/Target Challenge/word-notification path. Basic Attack does not activate the presentation and retains its existing single visual. Enemy damage impacts retain a 6x root scale for other attacks; Medium Attack scopes its enemy-owned impact burst to 9x (50% larger) without changing damage or hit detection.
- Medium-only presentation doubles the additive billboard RGB and the dedicated `Hovl_SoftSphericalGlow` particle RGB on each runtime orb instance; Basic Attack's source orb prefab and brightness remain unchanged. Each orb now receives the two authored TrailRenderers from `Assets/Vefects/Trails VFX URP/VFX/Particles/VFX_Trail_Electric.prefab`: the white-cyan inner filament (`M_VFX_URP_Trail_Electric_01`) and wider electric-blue outer ribbon (`M_VFX_URP_Trail_Electric_02`). Runtime instances retain the imported animated URP shader/material appearance, disable the package demo AudioSource and distortion layer, and use tuned lifetimes (0.45s inner / 0.60s outer) and widths (0.24 / 0.38). The trail roots follow the corresponding orb independently of the orb's 7.5 visual scale, detach and fade after accepted impact, and are cleaned up after their longest trail lifetime. Basic Attack's source orb prefab and brightness remain unchanged.
- Medium Attack damage now includes a capped actual-travel-distance bonus. The single logical projectile accumulates movement distance after its launch visibility hold; ordinary Medium hits receive no bonus through 4 travelled units, ramp linearly to +30% at 24 units, then remain capped. Target Challenge casts are explicitly excluded so their barrier-only resolution remains unchanged. The bonus is applied after any vocabulary-barrier damage adjustment and before the enemy damage receiver.

# SPELLSTRIKE Prologue Canva Dialogue Script

## Purpose

This is the working text-placement script for the 21 flattened 1920 x 1080
Prologue pages. Canva owns the final narration boxes, speech balloons, tails,
lettering, and page composition. Unity displays the exported page images.

Text marked **Locked** is either already approved in the panel manifest or was
explicitly settled during Canva page assembly. Text marked **Proposed** requires
approval before final Canva export.
Silent panels should remain silent; do not add filler dialogue merely to occupy
empty space.

## Page 1

**Source:** Panel 1  
**Layout:** Full-page peaceful family memory

**Locked narration:**

> Once upon a time, a wizard named Van Aert lived peacefully with his daughter,
> Nevali...

**Placement:** One restrained narration box in the upper-left dark area. Keep
Van and Nevali unobstructed.

## Approval Notes

- Locked text must not be paraphrased during Canva assembly.
- The complete Panel 26 message must remain exactly as written.
- Ellipses and dash styles may be normalized typographically in Canva, but the
  wording and speaker ownership must remain unchanged.
- Proposed text becomes production-locked only after explicit approval.
- If a balloon competes with a face, hand, staff, portal, message, or bridge,
  revise the balloon placement or shorten the proposed copy; do not crop the
  approved visual evidence.

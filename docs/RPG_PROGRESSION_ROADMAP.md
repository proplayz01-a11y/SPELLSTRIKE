## Approved direction

- Existing blue EXP HUD artwork and SetExperienceFraction binding; do not replace the approved HUD.
- Persistent Van level and EXP; grant EXP once per enemy death. Preserve vocabulary/combat mechanics.
- User revision (2026-11-25): Level/EXP persistence is enabled only for a campaign session explicitly started or continued from the Main Menu. Carry that session authorization through stage transitions; entering Play Mode directly in a gameplay scene must not load or save campaign level/EXP. Direct-scene tests still support temporary EXP, leveling, and derived stat growth, discarded when the test session ends. Default to nonpersistent progression unless the Main Menu authorizes campaign persistence. This revision concerns leveling/EXP; other existing save systems are unchanged by this design decision.
- Initial implemented, adjustable balancing: level cap 20, next-level threshold 80 + 20*(level-1), +10 maximum HP and +5% of base word damage per level. Enemy EXP: regular 35/45/55/65 and major boss 80/100/120/140 by stages 1–4. Tutorial/minigames grant no EXP. A freshly spawned enemy may reward EXP again on a stage replay; a single enemy instance cannot award twice. These are implementation defaults, not final campaign balancing. Level-up HP changes preserve missing HP and do not revive a dead player. Vocabulary-only damage remains unmodified.
- Increasing enemy difficulty from Stage 1 through the final stage; do not make bosses merely damage sponges.
- Generate distinct boss HP artwork after EXP is implemented. Overhead, camera-facing boss bars rather than a large screen-fixed bar.
- Potion drops after enemy defeat; collect into existing inventory, never automatically consume. Proposed rates 35/60/80/100% by encounter tier, subject to balancing.
- Fragment visual-only scale: 1.08 * 1.12 = 1.2096 of original. Preserve pickup colliders and interaction radius.
- Preserve both JSON and SQLite save compatibility and existing progress. No duplicate death rewards.

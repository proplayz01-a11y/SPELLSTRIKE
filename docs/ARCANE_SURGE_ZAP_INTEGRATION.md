## Runtime design, deliberately independent of vendor source

1. `AttackController.OnStrongAttackZapRelease` launches `HomingProjectile` through the same existing `ShootMagicProjectile` logic at the **unchanged** animation release frame. `OnAttackRelease` remains intact for other tiers. Only **StrongAttack, 10–13 letters**, and **not Target Challenge** attempts `EnableArcaneSurge()`.
2. `ArcaneSurgePresentation` reads the current target head/bounds each Update and moves the projectile's logical tip from **18 world units above the live head to the live head in precisely 0.20 seconds** (extended from 11 world units to better match the enormous Vefects sample screenshot). During this interval the old orb visual/collider is off and the overhead tracer tracks the target's changing X/Z and head height.
3. `ArcaneSurgeZapVisual` is independent of the projectile's lifetime; it draws a short bright/soft tracer during descent. `OnStrongZapYellow` authorizes the first `HomingProjectile.ResolveArcaneSurgeContact` after 0.20s of descent, and `OnStrongZapDarkBlue` / `OnStrongZapCyanBlue` trigger the remaining real damage + visual strikes **from their own animation frames**, normally 0.20s apart. There is no Zap impact scheduling coroutine.
4. The original `HomingProjectile.ResolveAcceptedImpact` remains the only owner of accepted-contact authority, the vocabulary barrier calculation and one word/Spellbook notification. It queues the **single approved damage total** and captured receiver on the independent Zap root. `ArcaneSurgeZapVisual` calls the same enemy receiver's original `TakeDamage` once at each strike, dividing that approved total into three integer amounts. It NEVER repeats `ResolveAcceptedImpact`, `VocabularyBarrier.ResolveHit`, or the word notification. No Zap particle prefab owns a gameplay collider.
5. First hit uses the target's current head position and original collider ground bounds. For the two later visual hits, a still-active target's head (humanoid bone if present, otherwise collider top) and feet bounds are refreshed. If the first hit kills/despawns the enemy, later visual zaps use the **last accepted contact** rather than an invalid transform. Vendor child objects `Zap Add Floor` and `Scorch` play near the enemy's feet. This adaptation requires visual acceptance, especially for unusual rigs.
6. Vendor audio `SFX_Vefects_Zap_Big_02.wav` plays at each of the three visual impacts with limited volume, alongside short camera impulses. All vendor Lights are disabled at runtime; imported materials are never edited.
7. The three requested color styles **play in sequence within ONE Strong Attack**: Yellow → Dark Blue → Cyan Blue, separated by **0.20 seconds** each. These are **three actual HP damage hits** using one approved total; e.g. 30 → 10/10/10. If a target is killed after an earlier strike, later strikes still play visual/audio at the last accepted position but do not damage a corpse. Enemy-owned hit effects and boss-specific phase transitions can also occur on each damage call, so the gameplay impact of multiple hit reactions must be validated. Up to three overlapping vendor prefabs (each 9 Particle Systems) require device profiling.

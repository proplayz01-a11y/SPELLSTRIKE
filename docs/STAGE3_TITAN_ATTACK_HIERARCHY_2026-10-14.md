## Design rule: no randomized move selection

All distances below are **horizontal world metres from Titan root to Van root**, not damage radii. Selectors require completed Awakening, live Van, valid NavMesh, no active attack, cooldown readiness and the relevant Animator states. The `TitanEarthbreakerController` owns every state change and no move can interrupt another merely because Van crosses a band during its windup. Source: `Assets/Stage 3/Scripts/TitanAttackDecision.cs` pure `EvaluateHierarchy`, invoked by `TitanEarthbreakerController` only when `enableAttackHierarchy=true`.

### Anti-spam, commitments and fairness

#### Earthbreaker Slam anti-repeat rule (October 14 follow-up)

**Enabled in actual production Titan prefab**, via `enableSlamAntiRepeat=true` and `slamAntiRepeatMoveMeters=10` together with `enableAttackHierarchy=true`. This specifically fixes the observed identical Slam at ~25.38m after every 2.5s recovery. When an authentic Slam actually STARTS, it sets `slamAntiRepeatLocked` and remembers Titan's world XZ. A second Slam is forbidden until either:

1. A **different authentic Hammerfist or V017 animation actually starts**, clearing the lock; or
2. Titan physically travels at least **10.0 world metres in XZ on the real NavMesh** after that Slam. Moving Van, waiting through cooldown, turning, NavMesh vertical baseOffset, an invalid path, or having the Animator return to Hold do NOT count.

While locked in the Slam-only 20–34m or 50–65m bands, the deterministic chooser returns **Reposition** instead of Slam. The real NavMeshAgent uses approved `ColossalHeavyWalk` to navigate toward the ~41.53m original Hammerfist contact band (back away if too close, approach if too far), with 0.5m stopping distance and a fully computed NavMesh path. If directly blocked, it tries a small set of grounded side-angle waypoints; if none produces a complete path it logs a throttled `[TITAN ANTI-REPEAT]` warning and **keeps Slam locked** rather than teleporting or fabricating movement. Hammerfist's actual facing/event/collider/cooldown gates still apply; V017's native anatomy and 4/4 LBS checks are unchanged. When it has walked 10m, Slam is eligible again but only if its existing safety and recovery constraints pass. Inspector `slamAntiRepeatStatus` and the `[TITAN ANTI-REPEAT]` logs show lock/unlock causes.

Anti-repeat is opt-in so other legacy Titan scenes keep their original attack behavior. `TitanAttackDecision.HasRepositionedSinceSlam` evaluates actual horizontal Titan coordinates and fails closed on nonfinite points. Pure Editor QA now has **25** deterministic cases including stationary 25m lock, mid-range Hammerfist alternative, far-band reposition, cooldown, 9.99m rejection, exactly 10m acceptance, vertical-only movement rejection and invalid coordinates.

## The One Library Rule

Panels set in the library must depict **one building**, not a newly invented wizard library per image. Camera angles may change, but the spatial relationships below must remain believable.

## Canonical Floor Plan

```text
                 REAR ARCHIVE ROOM
       tall shelves / back doors / quieter research area
                           |
                    central archway
                           |
                 red-carpet passage
                           |
     LEFT SHELVES       STUDY CHAMBER       RIGHT SHELVES
                       main wooden table
                           |
      stained-glass window at lower center; exterior door at lower right
```

### Fixed Zones

| Zone | Required landmarks | Narrative use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exterior entry | Lower-right exterior door; the lower-center facade holds a stained-glass window between blue banners | Arrival, hesitation, transition into the study chamber |
| Study chamber | Main wooden table on the central rug; overhead chandelier; shelving on both sides | Conversations, confession, book examination, magical contact |
| Central passage | Stone arch beyond the study room; red carpet continues through it | Movement, discovery, linking study to archive |
| Rear archive | More secluded research room behind the arch; additional shelves and back doors | Ancient-book discovery, deeper research, private revelation |
| Side shelving | Tall bookcases and small study objects on both sides of the main route | Depth framing only; never replace the central circulation route |

## Continuity Laws

1. The main table belongs in the front study chamber, before the central arch.
2. The arch and red-carpet route connect the study chamber to the rear archive.
3. Shelving flanks the route; it does not turn the library into an undefined wall of books with no clear spatial exit.
4. A shot facing toward the rear can show the arch behind the table; a shot facing toward the front can show the entry beyond the table. Both must still imply the same room.
5. Rear-archive shots must retain a believable connection back through the arch/passage rather than becoming a separate unrelated library.
6. Do not add a second grand hall, arbitrary throne room, portal chamber, or new architecture unless a future story panel explicitly requires it.

## Approved Visual Treatment

Use Panel 11 as the style target:

- cinematic fantasy-comic illustration;
- clean, confident dark outlines;
- controlled painterly/cel-shaded surfaces;
- readable warm amber practical light against restrained navy/cyan shadow;
- teal, navy, gold, and cyan accents kept deliberate rather than scattered;
- visual depth through foreground shelf/table framing, midground characters, and a legible arch or room boundary behind them.

### Lighting Is Beat-Specific, Not Building-Specific

| Emotional beat | Lighting direction |
| --- | --- |
| Van arrives grieving | cooler, subdued ambient light; limited warm practical lamps |
| Van confesses the loss | warm table/candle focus surrounded by darker room depth |
| Research or dream discussion | amber study light with controlled cyan magical accents only when justified |
| Nevali contact | Panel 11's warm-gold/cyan magical contrast; no unrelated violet corruption |

## Character and Prop Constraints

- **Older Van Aert:** white half-tied hair, tapered white beard, glasses, blue eyes, lean aged build, navy-and-gold robe.
- **Vander Poel:** loose white/grey hair, broad white beard, glasses, teal mantle with gold trim, blue diamond chest gem, frail scholarly posture.
- **Van's staff:** dark-brown wooden shaft, engraved gold fittings, one symmetric light-cyan diamond crystal. No additional branches, gems, metal shaft, or dark-blue crystal.
- **Nevali:** brown braided hair, blue bow, blue eyes, cream/blue/gold dress.

## Prompt Guardrails for Every Regenerated Library Panel

Include all of the following in each prompt:

```text
same canonical SPELLSTRIKE library established by the top-down layout;
front study chamber, main table before the central arch, flanking shelves,
red-carpet route leading through the arch to the rear archive;
retro educational fantasy-comic cutscene style matching approved Panel 11;
landscape 16:9; no embedded text, speech bubbles, captions, watermark,
or random writing
```

Then add only the approved panel-specific characters, action, emotion, camera angle, and lighting direction.

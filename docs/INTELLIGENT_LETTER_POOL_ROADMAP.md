## Implementation and controlled QA — 2026-11-25
- New IntelligentLetterPoolPlanner indexes 19,745 eligible words from the 21,004-entry curated dictionary. Index built once on dictionary loading. Sparse per-word letter counts, bounded length/difficult-letter samples, priority shortlists and pair scoring reuse existing letters before generating missing ones. Normal plans favor varied medium/strong lengths, with rare ultimate opportunities; prefer reuse without forcing every leftover into a word. New-letter output is shuffled and fills only the available replacement budget. Planned words are independently verified by construction as a simultaneous letter multiset; the second word may be empty when no compatible pair is found. No new runtime network or AI dependency.
- TileManager uses the planner for actual normal combat attack refills in persistent stage scenes. Selectable retained tiles and pending world replacements are inputs; locked/broken letters occupy capacity but are not used to claim a playable word. Existing tile objects, state, counters, collection mechanics and combat damage are untouched. Debug Main/Remainder labels now describe the new projected pair for contextual refills rather than a permanently fixed-length slot. Forecast assumes the planned pickups spawn and are collected; not a guarantee that the words are immediately available. Legacy fallback remains. Fixed an old two-word fallback bug where the second complete word was skipped once the first was added.
- Stage 2 uncompleted exact-length and distinct maritime objectives take priority. Tutorial and authored Stage 3 riddle generation keep their existing paths; temporary vocabulary challenge pools and exact normal-pool restoration remain isolated. Full Stage 3/4 runtime replay pending.
